01Context

Pharmacies, hospitals and distributors order pharmaceutical products in volume. B2B buyers behave very differently from retail shoppers: they know what they want, reorder often and need accuracy above all.

02Problem

Design an ordering experience that makes finding the right product fast, supports different buyer types, and keeps the ordering workflow streamlined and error-resistant.

03My role

UI/UX Designer — product discovery, ordering flow, role-based experiences and the visual design for a web build.

  • Figma
  • WordPress / Web
  • Claude AI

04Research & understanding

  • Defined the buyer types (pharmacy, hospital, distributor) and how their needs differ.
  • Mapped the ordering journey from discovery to cart to order confirmation.
  • Studied how pharmaceutical products are identified and searched for.

05UX process

  1. 01Designed product discovery around search and structured filters.
  2. 02Streamlined the ordering workflow for quick, repeatable B2B orders.
  3. 03Designed role-based variations of the experience.
  4. 04Created a responsive layout for desktop and mobile ordering.

06Wireframes

Wireframes for catalogue, product detail, cart and checkout — focused on speed and clarity over merchandising.

07Visual design

A professional, trustworthy visual language suited to a pharmaceutical brand, with clear product information and quantities.

08Prototype

A clickable ordering flow used to validate the journey before implementation.

09Development

Designs prepared for implementation with WordPress and web technologies.

10Result

  • A focused B2B ordering journey from discovery to checkout.
  • Experiences tailored to different buyer roles.
  • A responsive portal ready for web implementation.

11Learnings

B2B commerce rewards efficiency over persuasion. Removing steps mattered more than adding features.
